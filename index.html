05 · FAQ

Questions

Short answers to what people ask before they install.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no trial and no account. Download it and use it.

Does it send my voice anywhere?

No. Speech recognition happens on your computer. Murmur has no servers and doesn't collect anything.

What does Use AI do, and is it required?

It's optional and off by default. On the Mac you can add your own API key and Murmur will polish each dictation with an LLM in the tone you choose. Your audio never leaves your computer; only the transcribed text goes to the provider you picked. If the service is slow or offline, Murmur types its own offline version instead.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, on Windows, to download the speech model (about 190 MB) the first time you open it. After that, both versions work fully offline.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. What do I do?

Murmur isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS says it can't verify the app. What do I do?

Murmur isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS shows that warning. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Murmur, then confirm. You only do this once.

How do I change the key?

On the Mac, open Settings from the menu bar icon. If you use fn, set System Settings → Keyboard → “Press 🌐 key to” → Do Nothing. On Windows, right-click the tray icon and choose Push-to-talk key.

How do I uninstall it on Windows?

Right-click the tray icon and choose Uninstall Murmur, or remove it from Settings → Apps → Installed apps. It also deletes the speech model.
